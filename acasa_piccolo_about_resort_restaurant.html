Who we are

Hospitality with a more personal touch.

Acasa Piccolo brings together the comfort of a welcoming resort and the pleasure of dining together. We want guests to feel at ease from the moment they arrive, whether they are checking in for a relaxing stay, meeting loved ones for a meal, or visiting us for a special occasion.

Our approach is simple: thoughtful hospitality, inviting spaces, attentive service, and food enjoyed in good company. The resort offers a place to unwind, while our restaurant gives guests and visitors another reason to gather, celebrate, and make memories.

For us, hospitality is about the whole experience: how you are welcomed, how comfortably you rest, the care behind a meal, and the memories you take with you.
